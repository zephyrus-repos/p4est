\hypertarget{simple_2simple2_8c-example}{}\doxysection{simple/simple2.\+c}
This application (3D counterpart\+: \mbox{\hyperlink{simple_2simple3_8c-example}{simple/simple3.\+c}}) generates, refines, and adjusts a mesh set on the command line.\+The resulting VTK files visually present each stage of the mesh modification procedure.

Usage\+: \begin{quote}
{\ttfamily p4est\+\_\+simple $<$configuration$>$ $<$level$>$} \end{quote}
possible configurations\+:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item unit Refinement on the unit square.
\item brick Refinement on a regular forest of octrees.
\item three Refinement on a forest with three trees.
\item evil Check second round of refinement with np=5 level=7
\item evil3 Check second round of refinement on three trees
\item pillow Refinement on a 2-\/tree pillow-\/shaped domain.
\item moebius Refinement on a 5-\/tree Moebius band.
\item star Refinement on a 6-\/tree star shaped domain.
\item cubed Refinement on a 6-\/tree cubed sphere surface.
\item disk Refinement on a 5-\/tree spherical standard disk.
\item xdisk Refinement on a 5-\/tree spherical disk periodic in x.
\item ydisk Refinement on a 5-\/tree spherical disk periodic in y.
\item pdisk Refinement on a 5-\/tree spherical disk, periodic b.\+c.
\item periodic Refinement on the unit square with all-\/periodic b.\+c.
\item rotwrap Refinement on the unit square with weird periodic b.\+c.
\item circle Refinement on a 6-\/tree donut-\/like circle.
\item drop Refinement on a 5-\/trees geometry with an inner hole.
\item icosahedron Refinement on the sphere
\item shell2d Refinement on a 2d shell with geometry.
\item disk2d Refinement on a 2d disk with geometry.
\item bowtie Refinement on a 2-\/tree bowtie domain.
\end{DoxyItemize}
